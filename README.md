# Articulate: AI English Voice Tutor & Phonetic Coach

A voice-first, dual-channel AI English tutoring application engineered for real-time conversational mastery and sub-word phonetic precision.

- **Pronunciation & Accent Gym (ELSA Mode)**: Sub-word IPA phoneme breakdown, articulatory guides, minimal-pair drills.
- **High-Cadence Speaking Drills (Speak Mode)**: Quick-fire stimulus-response sentence pattern workouts.
- **Immersive Roleplays & Debates (Loora / Praktika Mode)**: Goal-oriented professional & social workplace simulations.
- **Dual-Channel Feedback**: Conversational voice stream + visual phoneme accuracy scoring & metalinguistic recasting.
- **Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS / SM-2)**: Local memory tracking weak sounds and vocabulary.
